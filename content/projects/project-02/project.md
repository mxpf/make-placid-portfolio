# Holland & Knight

Visual identity expansion and system design

For Holland & Knight, I developed a flexible visual system around the firm’s established logo and wordmark. A new radiating-line pattern, expanded color palette, typography, photography direction, and layout principles gave the identity greater distinction while preserving the clarity and credibility expected of a global law firm.

I translated the system into the tools people used every day—design exhibits, presentations, advertising, digital and social communications, newsletters, invitations, stationery, and promotional materials—creating a coherent foundation for both formal and expressive applications.
