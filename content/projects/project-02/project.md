# Holland & Knight

Visual direction and system foundation

For Holland & Knight, I built a flexible visual system around the firm’s established logo and wordmark—extending color, typography, photography, pattern, and layout into practical tools for a global legal organization.

The work spanned design exhibits, presentations, advertising, digital communications, social media, newsletters, invitations, stationery, and promotional materials—creating greater distinction without sacrificing clarity, credibility, or everyday usability.
