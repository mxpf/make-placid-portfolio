# Sands

Two directions for a global ESG reporting system

As report design lead for a competitive Sands ESG reporting pitch, I developed two visual directions that translated a complex responsibility platform into clear editorial systems.

“Details of Progress” connected operational precision to measurable outcomes, while “Places That Thrive” used maps, pathways, and local proof points to show how each resort contributes to the communities around it.
