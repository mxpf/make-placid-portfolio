# IBM

Enterprise communications, digital products, motion, and live experiences

At VSA Partners, I served as Executive Creative Director for IBM, setting creative strategy and directing multidisciplinary teams across enterprise communications, digital products, motion, and live experiences. The work turned complex organizational and technology initiatives into clear narratives and useful tools—helping IBMers understand, adopt, and act on new ideas. Alongside directing the broader account, I contributed directly to the concept and development of several motion pieces shown here.
